About Carlor ESTech

An engineering-driven automotive electronics company from the Stuttgart region, focused on smart lighting for vehicle interiors and exteriors.

Who we are

Carlor ESTech GmbH was founded in 2024 in Frickenhausen, Baden-Württemberg — in the middle of Germany's automotive cluster. Our team combines experience in automotive hardware development, embedded software and lighting technology.

Our focus is the development and manufacturing of smart RGB LED modules and PCBs for mobile interior and exterior lighting. We cover the complete spectrum of modern automotive electronics: hardware development, EMC-compliant design, application software and bus communication — and deliver finished, tested modules to our customers.

Since 2024 we are a licensee of NARVA, one of the traditional German brands in automotive lighting, and we distribute our own products under the registered trademark Carlor®.

Business scope

What we are registered and set up to do

Development

Design of vehicle lighting systems, LED driver electronics and control units — hardware, firmware and optics.

Manufacturing

Production of lighting modules and PCBs via qualified partner EMS lines, with final assembly and end-of-line testing under our responsibility.

Distribution

Sale of lighting systems and control units to OEMs, Tier suppliers, special-vehicle builders and the aftermarket; import and export of vehicle components.

Consulting & software

Technical consulting in the automotive sector, and development of software for vehicle electronics.

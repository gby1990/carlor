Lighting modules & control electronics

Our portfolio covers ready-to-install lighting modules for interior and exterior applications as well as the electronics behind them — LED driver boards and bus-connected lighting control units. All products are developed in Germany and can be customised to your vehicle platform.

Interior

Roof & panorama lighting

Lighting modules for panoramic glass roofs, sunroof frames and roof consoles. Edge-lit designs create a homogeneous light frame around the roof; starlight headliners with fibre optics create a premium night-sky effect controlled from the vehicle HMI.

  • Homogeneous illumination even for long light edges

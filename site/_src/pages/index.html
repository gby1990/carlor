What we do

One partner for the complete lighting electronics stack

We cover the full spectrum of modern automotive electronics — hardware development, EMC-compliant layout, application software and bus communication — and bring it into production as ready-to-install modules.

Hardware development

Schematic design, PCB layout, LED driver topologies and power management for 12 V / 24 V and 48 V vehicle electrical systems.

  • Constant-current LED drivers
  • Addressable RGB / RGBW channels
  • Thermal & derating design

EMC-compliant design

Layouts and filtering designed for automotive EMC requirements (CISPR 25, ISO 7637, ISO 16750) from the first prototype onwards.

  • Filter & protection concepts
  • Pre-compliance measurements
  • Load-dump / transient robustness

Embedded software

Application firmware for lighting nodes: colour mixing, dimming curves, animations, diagnostics and bootloader-based updates.

  • Bare-metal & RTOS-based firmware
  • Colour calibration & binning compensation
  • UDS diagnostics

Bus communication

Integration into the vehicle network via LIN and CAN / CAN FD — as slave nodes, master gateways or stand-alone controllers.

  • LIN 2.x slave / master
  • CAN / CAN FD
  • Custom protocols for retrofit systems

Company

Engineering start-up with automotive DNA — located in the Stuttgart region

Carlor ESTech GmbH was founded in 2024 in Frickenhausen, in the heart of Germany's automotive cluster between Stuttgart and the Swabian Alb. We combine hardware, software and manufacturing know-how for automotive lighting and are a licensee of the traditional NARVA lighting brand.

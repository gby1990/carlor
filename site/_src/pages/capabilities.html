Capabilities

Engineering & manufacturing from one source

We cover the entire spectrum of modern automotive electronics — from hardware development and EMC-compliant design through application software to bus communication — and take our designs into series production.

Hardware development

  • System architecture and component selection (automotive-qualified, AEC-Q100/Q101/Q200)
  • Schematic design and multilayer PCB layout, rigid, flex and rigid-flex
  • LED driver topologies: linear, buck, boost, buck-boost, matrix
  • Power management for 12 V / 24 V / 48 V systems, reverse-polarity, load-dump protection
  • Thermal simulation and derating concepts
  • DFM / DFT reviews with our manufacturing partners

EMC-compliant design

  • Design according to CISPR 25, ISO 7637-2/-3, ISO 16750-2, ISO 11452
  • Filter, layout and grounding concepts for switched-mode LED drivers
  • In-house pre-compliance measurements (conducted emissions, transients)
  • Support of OEM-specific EMC test plans at accredited laboratories

Embedded software

  • Firmware for 8/16/32-bit automotive MCUs in C
  • Colour management: calibration, binning compensation, gamma and dimming curves
  • Lighting scenario engine, animations, synchronisation of multiple nodes
  • Bootloader, flash update via LIN / CAN, UDS (ISO 14229) diagnostics
  • Configuration tools and test software (PC-based)

Bus communication

  • LIN 2.x slave and master nodes, LDF creation, sleep / wake-up handling
  • CAN / CAN FD nodes, network management, partial networking
  • Custom serial protocols for retrofit and special-vehicle systems
  • Integration tests with residual bus simulation

Prototyping & validation

  • Fast in-house sample builds (A / B / C samples) and board bring-up
  • Functional and electrical tests, photometric measurements
  • Environmental tests: temperature cycling, humidity, vibration via partner labs
  • Lifetime and lumen-maintenance evaluation

Series production

  • SMT / THT assembly on partner EMS lines in Germany and Asia
  • Potting, conformal coating, light-guide and housing assembly
  • End-of-line test with flash programming and traceability
  • Small and medium volumes, from a few hundred to tens of thousands of units per year
  • Change management, PPAP-style documentation and obsolescence management
Process

How a project runs

Specification

Requirement analysis, feasibility, system architecture and quotation.

Design

Hardware, firmware and optics developed in parallel; design reviews with the customer.

Prototyping

A-samples for function, B-samples in near-series design.

Validation

EMC pre-compliance, environmental and lifetime tests; C-samples from series tools.

Production

Release, ramp-up on EMS line, EOL test and delivery.

Lifecycle

Firmware updates, engineering changes and component obsolescence handling.
